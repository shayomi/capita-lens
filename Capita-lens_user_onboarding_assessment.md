**One thing I think would make Capita-Lens genuinely different**

After everything we\'ve discussed, I would **avoid making the onboarding
feel like a static questionnaire**.

Instead, make it conversational.

For example:

**\"Do you know your business credit score?\"**

If the answer is **No**, don\'t just move on. Explain briefly:

*\"Many business owners aren\'t aware of their score. While it\'s one
factor lenders may consider, it\'s only one part of a broader
assessment. Let\'s continue evaluating the other areas that can
influence funding decisions.\"*

Or if they answer **Yes** to having overdue HMRC liabilities:

*\"This doesn\'t necessarily prevent funding, but lenders commonly
consider outstanding tax obligations as part of their assessment. We\'ll
factor this into your readiness profile and identify actions that may
strengthen your position.\"*

This approach makes the onboarding itself part of the product\'s value.

lenders and investors typically think, while simultaneously building
