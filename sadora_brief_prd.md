**Sadora-Lens Product Requirements Brief (MVP)**

**1. Product Overview**

**What is Sadora-Lens?**

Sadora-Lens is a cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform that
helps Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) prepare for external
finance before approaching lenders or investors.

The platform enables businesses to conduct **continuous self-due
diligence** by assessing themselves against the broad range of
financial, operational, governance and compliance factors that are
commonly considered during commercial funding assessments.

Rather than relying on a single indicator such as a business credit
score, Sadora-Lens provides businesses with a holistic understanding of
their overall capital readiness.

The platform does **not** guarantee funding approval. Instead, it helps
businesses understand where they currently stand, identify weaknesses,
close evidence gaps and improve their readiness before submitting
funding applications.

**2. The Problem**

Most SMEs apply for funding without fully understanding how lenders
assess businesses.

Many business owners believe that:

-   a good credit score

-   healthy financial statements

-   profitable trading

are enough to obtain funding.

However, lenders typically evaluate a much broader combination of
factors, including:

-   business credit

-   financial performance

-   cash flow

-   affordability

-   repayment history

-   existing debt

-   overdraft management

-   CCJs

-   insolvency history

-   director history

-   Companies House compliance

-   governance

-   supporting documentation

-   funding purpose

-   business maturity

-   sector risk

-   operational resilience

Most SMEs only discover weaknesses after submitting an application.

By then it is often too late.

**3. The Solution**

Sadora-Lens gives SMEs the opportunity to assess themselves before a
lender does.

Instead of forcing businesses to use multiple disconnected services,
Sadora-Lens brings these readiness considerations into one intelligent
platform.

Users receive:

-   an overall Capital Readiness Profile

-   readiness scores across multiple categories

-   identified risks

-   missing evidence

-   weaknesses

-   prioritised recommendations

-   a practical improvement roadmap

The objective is to increase preparedness---not guarantee funding
approval.

**4. Core Innovation**

The platform is powered by a proprietary **Decision Intelligence
Framework**.

Instead of simply displaying financial data or credit information, the
framework evaluates multiple readiness indicators simultaneously to
generate a holistic view of a business\'s capital readiness.

The framework:

-   combines multiple data sources

-   analyses relationships between indicators

-   identifies decision-critical weaknesses

-   prioritises recommendations

-   continuously reassesses readiness as the business changes

**6. User Journey**

**Step 1**

User registers.

**Step 2**

Creates business profile.

Includes:

-   company name

-   registration number

-   sector

-   years trading

-   turnover

-   employees

**Step 3**

Chooses funding objective.

Examples:

-   £50,000 working capital

-   £250,000 expansion loan

-   Commercial mortgage

-   Investment

-   Grant funding

**Step 4**

Completes guided assessment.

Assessment sections include:

**Business Information**

**Financial Health**

**Credit & Risk**

**Governance**

**Compliance**

**Operational Readiness**

**Funding Objective**

**Step 5**

Framework performs assessment.

**Step 6**

Dashboard generated.

Displays:

Overall Capital Readiness

Category Scores

Risk Summary

Evidence Gaps

Priority Improvements

Readiness Timeline

**Step 7**

User works through recommendations.

Platform tracks progress.

**Step 8**

Business reassesses.

Profile updates continuously.

**12. Long-Term Vision**

Sadora-Lens should become the platform SMEs visit **before** approaching
external finance.

Rather than asking:

\"Can I get funding?\"

The platform helps businesses ask:

\"Am I genuinely ready for funding, and what should I improve before I
apply?\"

The long-term objective is to become the UK\'s leading Capital Readiness
platform, enabling SMEs to understand, assess and continuously improve
the broad range of factors commonly considered during commercial funding
assessments. Through its proprietary Decision Intelligence Framework,
Sadora-Lens aims to make funding preparation more transparent,
structured and accessible, helping businesses approach lenders and
investors with greater confidence and better-quality applications.
